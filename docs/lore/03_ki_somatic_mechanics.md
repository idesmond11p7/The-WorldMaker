# Universal Ontological Taxonomy: Ki (気)

## 1. Foundational Premise: Somatic Kinetic Constant
Ki is neither a spell nor a mental calculation. While higher-order occult arts alter Many-Worlds quantum superpositions through precognitive simulation, **Ki is the indigenous somatic and kinetic constant of physical biology**.

> *"It is the raw, un-warped pressure of living organisms asserting physical territory, structural mass, and kinetic velocity against an indifferent universe."*

Ki does not tax the mind with Possibility Resistance. It taxes the physical hardware: muscle meat, vascular walls, tendons, and marrow.

## 2. Section I: The Four Primal Types (Ichirei Shikon)

### 1. Ara-Ki (荒気 — The Ravaging / Kinetic Vector)
*   **Ontological Domain**: Pure forward momentum, destructive kinetic discharge, and territorial violence.
*   **Visual Signature**: Sanguine-crimson core fringed with unstable bruised-violet static and white-hot discharge arcing off physical extremities.
*   **Somatic Mechanism**: Massive cardiovascular overpressure; vascular channels expand to maximum tolerance; muscle fibers act as dense kinetic springs rejecting dampening forces.
*   **Environmental Behavior**: Triggers local barometric pressure crashes; outward atmospheric displacement repels loose gravel and dust radially from footing.

### 2. Nigi-Ki (和気 — The Structural / Cohesive Vector)
*   **Ontological Domain**: Biological equilibrium, structural mass retention, and tissue stabilization.
*   **Visual Signature**: Dense, vitrified bone-white and deep slate-grey forming a thin, mirror-clear boundary millimeters above the epidermis.
*   **Somatic Mechanism**: Circulates along periosteum, deep tendon sheaths, and fascial networks; clears waste, seals micro-fractures in bone under impact, and deadens excessive neuro-sensory trauma.
*   **Environmental Behavior**: Eliminates micro-vibrations in terrain; artificially maximizes ground friction (mud and gravel behave as solid traction bedrock).

### 3. Kushi-Ki (奇気 — The Instinctual / Prescient Vector)
*   **Ontological Domain**: Non-cognitive spatial intuition, predatory trajectory mapping, and feral awareness.
*   **Visual Signature**: Translucent phosphorescent amber and pale lynx-gold radiating as faint micro-filaments from sensory nodes.
*   **Somatic Mechanism**: Pupillary compression into vertical slits; inner ear recalibrates to acoustic stress and air displacement; neuromuscular pathways bypass conscious cerebral deliberation, firing directly via spinal cord and peripheral networks.
*   **Environmental Behavior**: Leaves zero acoustic wake; movement produces negligible air turbulence, evading seismic alerts.

### 4. Saki-Ki (幸気 — The Trophic / Predatory Siphon)
*   **Ontological Domain**: Metabolic assimilation, cellular reclamation, and vital siphoning.
*   **Visual Signature**: Viscous, light-swallowing oil-black laced with undulating veins of bruised plum.
*   **Somatic Mechanism**: Massive acceleration of endocrine and digestive reclamation; recycles external kinetic shock and metabolic waste into secondary fuel cycles.
*   **Environmental Behavior**: Draws moisture and thermal energy from immediate surroundings; ambient air chills rapidly; vegetation beneath feet desiccates into brittle dust.

## 3. Section II: The Five Operational Versions (Compression Gradient)

$$\text{Version 0: Gen-Ki} \longrightarrow \text{Version I: Ren-Ki} \longrightarrow \text{Version II: Gō-Ki} \longrightarrow \begin{cases} \text{Version III: Yō-Ki} \\ \text{Version IV: Mu-Ki} \end{cases}$$

1.  **Version 0: Gen-Ki (元気 — Metabolic Baseline)**: Passive biological maintenance in all organic life; fuels respiration and cellular mitosis; cannot be projected externally.
2.  **Version I: Ren-Ki (練気 — Coiled Somatic Tension)**: Compressed Ki circulated through disciplined breath-retention and tendon wrapping; grants sub-dermal impact armor and hydraulic linear acceleration without wind-up.
3.  **Version II: Gō-Ki (豪気 — Atmospheric Rupture)**: Extreme external territorial projection; heavy sanguine-indigo plumes venting from shoulders and spine; acoustic roar (Kiai) shatters structures and launches air-wedges striking yards beyond physical limbs.
4.  **Version III: Yō-Ki (妖気 — Inverted Bestial Warp)**: Primordial Ki radiated by apex chimeras and organisms linked to Inverted Beast Manifolds; triggers physical bone mutation, elongated spinal reflexes, and telluric ground-coupling (bleeding shock into bedrock).
5.  **Version IV: Mu-Ki (無気 — Null-Somatic Void)**: Total collapse of somatic presence into core marrow; organism registers as dead mass to thermal, seismic, olfactory, and bio-electric senses; attacks produce zero pre-strike atmospheric tell.

## 5. Section IV: The Somatic Hardware Engine & Biological Toll
Because Ki is entirely biological, overloading it destroys the mortal meat:

1.  **Thermal Exhaust (Pore-Steam Dissipation)**: Peak exertion spikes core body temperatures past 110°F (43°C). The body protects vital organs by violently venting superheated steam through pores, shrouding operators in hissing clouds of vaporized sweat.
2.  **Lactic Vitrification (Muscular Calcification)**: Exceeding capacity under Ara-Ki or Gō-Ki causes cellular respiration failure; un-cleared lactic acid and metabolic salts crystallize into brittle mineral lattices, locking muscles into rigid, stone-like paralysis.
3.  **Arterial & Capillary Blowout**: Sustained hydraulic backpressure fractures blood vessels, causing scleral hemorrhaging (eyes turning solid crimson), epistaxis, and massive subcutaneous hematomas across forearms and torso.
