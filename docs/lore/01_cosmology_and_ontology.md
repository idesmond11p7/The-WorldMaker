# The ForeverWorlds / Ur-Worlds: Cosmology & Ontological Taxonomy

## 1. Scope & Primary Source Foundation
This document records the foundational metaphysics, cosmic hierarchy, and existential tiers of the **ForeverWorlds / Ur-Worlds** cosmology, transcribed from the primary world-lore canon.

---

## 2. The Spectrum of Supernatural Existence
Supernatural energies and entities within the Ur-Worlds are classified across three distinct operational layers:

### A. Physical Matter States
Supernatural energies can manifest, interact, and stabilize into any physical state of matter:
*   Classical States: Solid, Liquid, Gas, Plasma.
*   Exotic Quantum States: Quark-Gluon Plasma, Degenerate Matter, Bose-Einstein Condensate, Fermionic Condensate, Superfluid, Supersolid, Time Crystals, Superionic Ice.

### B. Metaphysical Forms
Abstract and in-explainable by physical observation. Violates the "laws of infinite possibility" by being fundamentally impossible to physicality.
*   **Non-Physical & Non-Corporeal**: Completely distinct from merely being on a "different energy spectrum" or "different vibrational frequency."
*   **Time-Space Blindness**: Unrecognizable by time-space; time and space are blind to it.
*   **Mass & Gravitational Nullity**: Possesses literally zero mass; cannot be influenced or bound by gravity or physical interaction.
*   **Observable Universe Inaccessibility**: Cannot exist or be observed within the boundaries of the physical observable universe.

### C. Structural Modes of Supernatural Energies
Supernatural energies exist in six recognized structural states:
1.  **Fields**: Ambient, continuous distributions permeating space.
2.  **Reserves**: Concentrated pools held by vessels, objects, or locations.
3.  **Connected Networks**: Relational webs linking points, minds, or realms.
4.  **Essence**: Inherent, concentrated signatures of specific identities or concepts.
5.  **Realms of Energy**: Autonomous dimensional planes constituted purely of energy.
6.  **En-Grafted**: Artificially or ontologically bonded to physical or spiritual hosts.

## 4. The Bifurcated Soul Architecture (Soul 1 vs. Soul 2)
In the Ur-Worlds, a soul is not a singular, uniform spiritual orb. It is split into two radically different ontological structures:

### Soul 1: The Semi-Spiritual Temporal Anchor
*   **Ontological Domain**: Linear, temporal, semi-spiritual. Connects quantum particles, information particles, temporal crystals, and quantum foam.
*   **Function**: Houses an entity's destiny—the linear continuum of past, present, and probable futures. It does not contain all possibilities, but it allows them.
*   **Destruction Consequence**: **Supernatural Nuke**. Destroying a Soul 1 fractures local spacetime, weakens dimensional temporal mechanisms, and attracts exotic horrors (Chronostalkers, Paradox Hounds, Chronoclasts, Null-Timers, Epoch Ghouls).

### Soul 2: The Spiritual MWI Blueprint
*   **Ontological Domain**: Full spirituality; non-Euclidean, non-temporal, purely abstract.
*   **Function**: Exists as an indefinite collection of all realities and possibilities linked to a single point. It represents what could be across the Many-Worlds.
*   **Destruction Consequence**: Less physically violent locally, but ontologically horrific: it deletes the entity's possibility of existing across all Many-Worlds timelines. The Ur-Verse itself is indifferent to this deletion, though internal dimensions may experience deep-level temporal resets.
