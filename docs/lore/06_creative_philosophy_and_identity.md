# The Lorekeeper / Worldmaker: Creative Philosophy & Production Guide

## 1. Epistemological Classification

### A. Established Canon (Direct Primary Evidence)
1.  **Quantum Superposition Magic**: Magic in the Ur-Worlds is strictly Many-Worlds Interpretation (MWI) probability manipulation via gap induction and collapse, driven by precognitive simulations.
2.  **Somatic Grounding of Ki**: Ki operates as an un-warped somatic biological constant. It does not tax the mind with Possibility Resistance; it burns muscle, bone, and vascular channels.
3.  **The Military Reserve Gun System**: Magic requires dedicated fuels matching specific outcome chambers. Possibility Resistance determines cost, not physical mass.
4.  **Cacodictyology**: A rigorous 144-hierarchy system across 12 Roots structuring all negative, predatory, and infernal supernatural networks.
5.  **Demonic Invariants**: Demons are characterized by adventurousness, unpredictability, and complete ruinous ending rather than prolonged theatrical torture.

### B. Inferred Design Principles
1.  **Hard Pseudoscience as Mythic Foundation**: Mythological systems (Vedic, Kemetic, Mesoamerican, Solomonic, Norse) are re-anchored into cutting-edge theoretical physics (MWI, ZFC set theory, superionic ice, non-Euclidean geometry) and evolutionary biology.
2.  **Evolutionary Utilitarianism**: Moral concepts like "evil" and emotional states are modeled through evolutionary game theory, hardware overriding, and social tracking software.
3.  **Visceral Combat & Audio-Visual Contrast**:
    *   *Visuals*: Heavy contrasts—obsidian vs. smokeless hellfire; chalk-white Nigi-Ki barriers vs. bruised-violet Ara-Ki static; hissing pore-steam vs. crystalline frost.
    *   *Acoustics*: Sub-bass seismic thrums, sudden vacuum silences (Mu-Ki, Bast-Irkalla), shrieking vacuum scythe edges (Chiryoku), and reverberating bronze bell chimes.
4.  **Anti-Slop Nomenclature**: Names fuse ancient historical linguistic roots with precise structural and physical descriptors (e.g., *Pactional Jurisprudence*, *Decanic Vector Anchoring*, *Suffumigative Phantasmata*, *Lactic Vitrification*).

### C. Unresolved Creative Questions & Design Vectors
1.  **Spatial Inter-Realm Transit**: How do non-Category 7 entities cross between the Bio-Manifold, the Underworld Sectors, and the Inverted Beast Manifolds?
2.  **Mortality vs. Inversion**: When a Category 4 entity dies, what dictates whether their Soul 1 dissolves, reconstitutes as an Astral shade, or descends into the Diyu/Naraka judicial processing queues?
3.  **Visual Representation in 3D Production**: How should the non-Euclidean geometry and MWI superpositions be represented in realtime 3D engines (e.g., vertex displacement shaders, stencil buffers, ray-marched signed distance fields)?

## 2. Production Considerations: 3D, VFX & Game Feel

### A. VFX & Animation Directives
*   **Ki Discharges**: Must never look like generic anime glowing auras. They are physical fluid/gas simulations: high-pressure steam venting through micro-pores, violent barometric air distortion, and directional kinetic shock-wedges.
*   **The Atmospheric Curdle**: When summoning Phantasmata or suffumigative entities, smoke does not merely emit—it physically curdles, congealing into semi-rigid geometric silhouettes driven by acoustic barbarous chants.
*   **Vacuum Cuts**: Chiryoku strikes should carve negative-space slices through ambient air that collapse milliseconds later with wet, concussive implosions.

### B. Sound Design (Game Feel)
*   **Gō-Ki Kiai**: Deep sub-harmonic frequency sweep (30–60 Hz) that briefly attenuates all ambient high-frequency sounds, followed by an explosive acoustic shockwave.
*   **Mu-Ki Presence**: Absolute digital silence filter—cutting all ambient reverberation and footsteps to emphasize total sensory erasure.
*   **Pactional Injunctions**: Sharp metallic clicks of clockwork bronze and heavy stone stamps striking parchment.

### C. 3D Modeling & Material Shaders
*   **Kokotsu Ridges**: Sub-surface scattering shaders on graphite-black calcified bone ridges layered over human dermal maps.
*   **Ryū-Ketsu Scales**: Anisotropic metallic roughness maps for burnished copper and deep emerald scales resting beneath translucent outer epidermis layers.
*   **Dite & Tartaros Architecture**: Extreme brutalist monolithic silhouettes, matte obsidian materials with procedural basalt fracture normals, contrasted against strobing cold-blue volumetric light cones.
