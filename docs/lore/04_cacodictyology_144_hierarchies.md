# Cacodictyology & The Nethermost: The 144 Hierarchies

## 1. Foundational Concept: Cacodictyology
**Cacodictyology** is the overarching ontological science studying how connected networks and fields of negative, non-physical supernatural energy bind across the Bio-Manifold.

*   **The Nethermost** (The Deep-Sunk, Foundationless, Sunken, Enders, Under-Realmers): Not simply a "bad place," but an ontological inverted architecture anchored into the fabric of the Ur-Worlds.
*   **Netherarchesial Reality Warping**: A unique capability of Category X entities (Cat 2, 3, 5, 7) that exploits the evolutionary asymmetry of mortal brains ("Bad is stronger than good").

## 3. Demonic Taxonomy & Ender Nature
Demons are defined by three distinct behavioral invariants:
1.  **Adventurousness**: High ambition, active expansion across realms, and presence in mortal records.
2.  **Unpredictability**: Inversion of authority; wielding power that shatters established cosmic hierarchies.
3.  **Ender-Traits**: Demons do not torture to provide hope or extract concessions; **they end civilizations**. They leave complete ruins, obliterating structures and souls entirely.
