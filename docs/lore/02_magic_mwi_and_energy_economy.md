# MAGIC: Many-Worlds Alignment & The Supernatural Fuel Economy

## 1. Ontological Definition of MAGIC
In the Ur-Worlds cosmology:
$$\text{MAGIC} = \text{"Many-Worlds Alignment via Gap Induction \& Collapse"}$$

*   **Classical Mechanics Inability**: Magic cannot be explained by classical Newtonian physics; it operates exclusively at the quantum level through the **Many-Worlds Interpretation (MWI)**, wave-particle duality, and quantum superposition.
*   **The Principle of Chaotic Beginning**: Order is derived from repetitive chaos. At its lowest foundational level, existence devolves into mindless, ontologically random chaos. Magic manipulates the probabilities of this chaos.
*   **Terminology Distinction**: 
    *   `MAGIC`: The universal scientific acronym and overarching supernatural phenomenon of MWI gap alignment.
    *   `Magick` / `Magica`: Specialized, rule-dense, historically bounded ceremonial frameworks used by in-world practitioners (e.g., Hermeticism, Solomonic Juridical systems, Decanic vector anchoring).

## 2. Precognitive Simulation & Evolutionary Psychology
Magic is not powered by emotional loudness or wishful thinking; it is driven by **Precognitive Future Simulation**.

*   **Evolutionary Biology Foundation**: Emotions are not arbitrary feelings; they are hardwired physiological override programs (hardware optimization) and ancient social cognitive algorithms.
*   **The Threat-Prioritization Bias ("Bad is Stronger than Good")**:
    *   Evolutionary survival requires brains to prioritize simulating lethal threats over positive rewards. Missing an opportunity costs a minor bonus; failing to anticipate an ambush equals death.
    *   Consequently, Category 4 minds run vastly more detailed, weighted, and urgent precognitive simulations for negative, hostile, or fearful outcomes.
*   **The Netherbound Exploitation**: Netherbound entities (Category 5) exploit this evolutionary asymmetry. By cultivating dread, conflict, and despair, they hijack the hyper-dense precognitive simulation bandwidth of mortal civilizations to fuel catastrophic reality-warping.

## 3. The Energy Economy: Gun System vs. Mana Laser
The Ur-Worlds completely rejects the generic fantasy trope of a unified "Mana Bar" where generic energy is poured into spells.

### The Military Reserve Bank / Gun System
1.  **The Shell (The Outcome)**: An intended magical outcome is an empty physical chamber. It has no power or validity on its own.
2.  **The Bullet (The Ammunition)**: Every specific outcome requires a dedicated, distinct supernatural fuel reserve (e.g., *Aethelfire Flux* for spatial disruption, *Vaelburn Spark* for thermal-frictional compression). You cannot fire a spatial chamber with kinetic ammunition.
3.  **The Blueprint (Precognitive Simulation)**: The caster must construct a frame-perfect, hyper-detailed internal simulation of how the future event unfolds. The fuel forces that mental blueprint across the quantum divide into physical actuality.

## 4. The Law of Possibility Resistance
Energy cost is governed not by physical mass or scale, but by **Possibility Resistance**:
*   **Low Resistance (High Probability)**: If an event is already close to occurring naturally in the timeline (e.g., a cracked dam failing under flood pressure), the timeline yields easily. Fuel cost is practically zero.
*   **High Resistance (Improbability / Impossibility)**: If an event contradicts natural timeline progression (e.g., transmuting solid basalt into fresh water), Possibility Resistance spikes exponentially, demanding staggering fuel reserves and inflicting severe neural and temporal lag on the caster's biological hardware.
